Try Ritvo before anyone else

We are looking for sports fans to try the beta on TestFlight, on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV.

What Ritvo is. An app that brings your teams' scores, line-ups, stats and alerts together with the IPTV list you already subscribe to. Ritvo includes no channels or broadcasts: you add your own M3U or Xtream Codes list, or try a sample list if you don't have one yet.

What we ask of you

  • Use Ritvo for a few weeks with your matches and your teams.
  • Tell us what fails or what you miss, from the app itself (Settings › Help › Suggestions and Problems).
  • Don't forward the invitation: it is personal and goes to your email.

How it works

  1. You fill this in (under a minute).
  2. We invite people in batches, depending on the devices and countries we need to test.
  3. If you are in a batch, you get an email from TestFlight (Apple) to install the beta. It's free.
