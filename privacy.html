Privacy Policy

Effective 27 November 2026 · Applies to Ritvo for iPhone, iPad and Apple TV, its server (api.ritvo.app), the account website and the beta sign-up (ritvo.app). The Spanish version is the reference text.

In short: Ritvo doesn’t track you or sell data. Your lists and preferences stay on your device or travel encrypted, so we can’t read them. Ritvo has its own server that provides sports data without knowing who you are. The Ritvo account is optional: it asks for no email, phone number or name, only a nickname you make up, and you can delete it at any time from the app. The free version for iPhone and iPad shows non-personalised Google AdMob ads; with Ritvo Pro there are no ads.

Who is responsible

Ritvo is an app by Daniel. For any privacy question, write to soporte@ritvo.app.

What Ritvo is

Ritvo is a sports app and a player for IPTV lists. It includes no channels or broadcasts: it plays the lists you add, from your own subscription with your provider.

What stays on your device

  • Your IPTV lists (address, channels, categories and PIN). Each list’s username and password are kept in the device’s Keychain.
  • Your preferences: favourite channels, the teams and competitions you follow, channel history and settings.
  • Temporary copies: images (crests, logos, programme artwork) and, if you use the live buffer, a few minutes of the channel you are watching. They are removed automatically.
  • Crash and performance reports: when the app closes suddenly or runs slowly, the system (MetricKit, on iPhone and iPad) or the app itself (on Apple TV) leaves a technical report. It holds app and device data (version, system, memory, where the code failed), not personal data. It is kept only on your device; you can read and delete it in Settings › Diagnostics, and it reaches us only if you choose to share it.

The Ritvo account (optional)

It keeps your lists, favourites and settings on all your devices, Android included. Without an account, Ritvo works the same on each device.

  • What we keep: your nickname; for each device, a random identifier, its public key, its name (encrypted), the hash of its access token, its Apple notification token and when it was last used; the public keys of your passkeys; and your encrypted box with lists, favourites and settings.
  • End-to-end encryption: the box is encrypted on your devices with a key only they have. We keep blobs we cannot open: we don’t see your lists, their passwords or which channels you watch.
  • What we don’t keep: email, phone number, name, date of birth, location, viewing history, advertising identifier or your IP address in the database.
  • How long: while you use it. If no device connects for 18 months, it is deleted entirely. A deleted account’s nickname stays reserved for 30 days (we keep only its hash) so no one can impersonate it.
  • Deleting it: from the app on any device (Settings › Account › Delete Account), or from the account website with your passkey. It is deleted at once. Account backups are kept separately for 30 days; if one ever had to be restored, deleted accounts are deleted again.
  • Legal basis: performing the service you request when you create the account.

Suggestions and problems

From Settings › Help you can propose ideas, vote for other people’s and tell us about problems. It is voluntary and asks for no name or email.

  • What we keep: what you write; for problems, where it happens (type of device, model, system and Ritvo version) and which device you send it from; the category; the conversation with us; the screenshot and technical report only if you add them (version, device, system and latest crashes, never your lists, channels or passwords); and Apple’s notification token only if you ask us to tell you about the answer. To know which submissions and votes are yours we use a random key of your device (we keep its fingerprint) or, if you have a Ritvo account, the account’s identifier.
  • What is public: only the suggestions we approve, sometimes with the text touched up, and their vote count. Never who sent them or who voted. Problems are never public.
  • What we don’t keep: name, email, phone number, IP address (used only in memory to limit abuse), advertising identifier or your lists.
  • How long: published suggestions, while they are published; unpublished ones, 90 days; problems, 180 days after they are closed, and their screenshot and report, 30 days after they are closed. You can delete any of your submissions at once from “My Submissions”; a published suggestion stays without your authorship, because the votes are other people’s. Deleting your Ritvo account deletes its submissions and votes.
  • Legal basis: your consent when you send it, and our legitimate interest in improving Ritvo and dealing with what you tell us.

Beta sign-up

At ritvo.app/beta-en.html you can sign up to try Ritvo on TestFlight before it launches. It is voluntary and only the email and the devices you would use it on are required. It is the only part of Ritvo that asks for data that identifies you.

  • What we keep: your email; your name, if you give it; your country and language; the devices you would use, with their model and system if you write them; the sports and teams you follow; whether you have an IPTV list and its type (M3U or Xtream Codes), never the provider; how you watch matches today; what you expect from Ritvo; how you heard about us; the date, the version of the text you accepted and, later, the date we invited you.
  • What for: choosing whom to invite in each batch (by device and country), inviting you, and learning what the people who sign up expect from Ritvo. We don’t use it for advertising and don’t send newsletters.
  • Who receives it: if we invite you, your name and email are uploaded to Apple’s TestFlight, which sends you the invitation and handles that data under its privacy policy. Each sign-up also reaches us as an email notice, with your answers, in the Ritvo owner’s mailbox, which is on Gmail (Google, under its privacy policy). Apart from that, your answers don’t leave our server.
  • What we don’t keep: your IP address (used only in memory to limit abuse) or the name of your IPTV provider. There are no analytics or cookies in the form.
  • How long: until the beta ends and 12 months at most after you sign up; then it is deleted automatically. The server’s encrypted backups last 30 more days. If you write to soporte@ritvo.app from that email, we delete your sign-up and its notice at once. The notices are also deleted within the same period. You can leave TestFlight yourself from the TestFlight app, or ask us.
  • Legal basis: your consent, given by ticking the box, which you can withdraw at any time.

Camera

If you scan your TV to sync a match, the camera reads the scoreboard clock. Images are processed on the device and are never saved or sent.

Notifications

Reminders before a match are scheduled on your device. Your account’s security alerts (for example, “someone wants to sign in to your account”) come through Apple with each device’s notification token. Score Live Activities update through an Apple broadcast channel, with no data about you.

Match alerts (goals, cards, half-time, full time and line-ups). If you turn them on, your device registers with our server, with no account or personal data. We keep:

  • a random identifier created by the app (never the advertising identifier) and the hash of a secret, so that only your device can change its registration;
  • the Apple notification token and, if you let your team’s match appear on its own on the Lock Screen, the Live Activities start token;
  • which teams, competitions and matches you follow and which alerts you want for each, whether you hide scores (“No Spoilers”) and the alerts’ delay;
  • the match you are watching and how far behind your picture is, for 3 hours at most, so you are never alerted before you see it.

We don’t keep your name, email, IP address, language, lists or history. The registration is deleted when you turn alerts off or remove the permission, and after 60 days without the app connecting. Legal basis: performing the service you ask for when you turn alerts on.

Ritvo Pro

The subscription is handled by Apple with your Apple ID. Ritvo only receives, on your device, Apple’s confirmation that you are Pro; we never see your payment details. You can cancel it in your Apple ID settings, under Subscriptions.

Ads

The free version of Ritvo for iPhone and iPad shows discreet ads from Google AdMob: one in each match page and a card, labelled «Ad», every few rows of Matches. Never in the player, on a live channel or over the video. There are no ads on Apple TV.

  • Always non-personalised. We ask Google not to personalise any ad. Ritvo doesn’t ask to track you (App Tracking Transparency), so it has no access to your device’s advertising identifier and doesn’t follow you across apps or websites. We also turn off the SDK’s use of the device’s location and of the app’s own identifier for ads.
  • Consent. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, you will see Google’s consent message before the first ad. You may accept or not: if you don’t, you may still see limited ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Advertising › Ad Privacy Options.
  • What Google receives is in the table above. Ritvo sends it nothing of its own (not your lists, your channels or the matches you follow).
  • With Ritvo Pro there are no ads and neither of Google’s SDKs starts.

Links to other services

«Where to Watch» may link to a TV service’s website, sometimes with a Ritvo affiliate code. The link sends no data about you; what you do on that website is covered by its own policy.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell data. The only third party that receives data for advertising is Google, as explained under “Ads”.
  • We use no analytics or tracking tools, and there are no personalised ads.
  • The app doesn’t ask for data that identifies you, including from minors: the account is optional and only uses a nickname. We only ask for an email if you sign up for the beta.

Who helps us

  • Contabo GmbH (Germany, European Union) and Cloudflare: Contabo hosts the api.ritvo.app server in the European Union, and Cloudflare protects it. Cloudflare sees IP addresses and paths, not the encrypted content of accounts.
  • API-Sports: sells us the sports data. Our server requests it with no data about you.
  • Apple: iCloud, notifications, the Ritvo Pro purchase and TestFlight (the beta). Google: ads in the free version.

If an authority asked us for an account’s data, we could only hand over what we keep (above), never the content of your box, which we cannot open.

Reports you share with Apple

If “Share With App Developers” is on in your device’s settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. You choose this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Your rights

Without an account, we keep no data about you and there is nothing of ours to access, correct or delete (except your beta sign-up, if you made one: write to us to see, correct or delete it). With an account, you can see and remove your devices in Settings › Account and delete the whole account at once; for any other request for access, rectification, portability or objection, write to us. For the ads’ data, you can change your consent in Settings › Advertising and exercise your rights with Google through its privacy policy. To remove everything from Ritvo on your devices, delete the app and, if you use iCloud, its data in your Apple ID’s iCloud settings. For any question or complaint, write to soporte@ritvo.app. You can also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es) or your local authority.

Changes

If what Ritvo does with data changes, we will update this page and its date before releasing the version that changes it.
