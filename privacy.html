Privacy Policy

Effective 14 November 2026 · Applies to Ritvo for iPhone, iPad and Apple TV. The Spanish version is the reference text.

In short: Ritvo has no accounts, no server of its own and no tracking. What you set up stays on your device or in your private iCloud, encrypted, where we can’t read it. The free version for iPhone and iPad shows non-personalised ads from Google AdMob, which receives some technical data to show them; Ritvo Pro has no ads.

Who is responsible

Ritvo is an app by Daniel. For any privacy question, write to soporte@ritvo.app.

What Ritvo is

Ritvo is a sports app and a player for IPTV lists. It includes no channels or broadcasts: it plays the lists you add, from your own subscription with your provider.

What stays on your device

  • Your IPTV lists (address, channels, categories and PIN). Each list’s username and password are kept in the device’s Keychain.
  • Your preferences: favourite channels, the teams and competitions you follow, channel history and settings.
  • Temporary copies: images (crests, logos) and, if you use the live buffer, a few minutes of the channel you are watching. They are removed automatically.
  • Crash and performance reports: when the app closes suddenly or runs slowly, the system (MetricKit, on iPhone and iPad) or the app itself (on Apple TV) leaves a technical report. It holds app and device data (version, system, memory, where the code failed), not personal data. It is kept only on your device; you can read and delete it in Settings › Diagnostics, and it reaches us only if you choose to share it.

Camera

If you scan your TV to sync a match, the camera reads the scoreboard clock. Images are processed on the device and are never saved or sent.

Notifications

Match reminders are scheduled on your device. We use no notification server.

Ritvo Pro

The subscription is handled by Apple with your Apple ID. Ritvo only receives, on your device, Apple’s confirmation that you are Pro; we never see your payment details. You can cancel it in your Apple ID settings, under Subscriptions.

Ads

The free version of Ritvo for iPhone and iPad shows discreet ads from Google AdMob: one in each match page and a card, labelled «Ad», every few rows of Matches. Never in the player, on a live channel or over the video. There are no ads on Apple TV.

  • Always non-personalised. We ask Google not to personalise any ad. Ritvo doesn’t ask to track you (App Tracking Transparency), so it has no access to your device’s advertising identifier and doesn’t follow you across apps or websites. We also turn off the SDK’s use of the device’s location and of the app’s own identifier for ads.
  • Consent. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, you will see Google’s consent message before the first ad. You may accept or not: if you don’t, you may still see limited ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Advertising › Ad Privacy Options.
  • What Google receives is in the table above. Ritvo sends it nothing of its own (not your lists, your channels or the matches you follow).
  • With Ritvo Pro there are no ads and neither of Google’s SDKs starts.

Links to other services

«Where to Watch» may link to a TV service’s website, sometimes with a Ritvo affiliate code. The link sends no data about you; what you do on that website is covered by its own policy.

What we don’t do

  • We have no user accounts and no database with your data.
  • We don’t sell data. The only third party that receives data for advertising is Google, as explained under «Ads».
  • We use no analytics or tracking tools, and there are no personalised ads.
  • We ourselves collect no one’s data, children’s included.

Reports you share with Apple

If “Share With App Developers” is on in your device’s settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. You choose this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Your rights

Since we keep no data about you, there is nothing of ours to access, correct or delete. For the ads’ data, you can change your consent in Settings › Advertising and exercise your rights with Google through its privacy policy. To remove everything from Ritvo, delete the app and, if you use iCloud, its data in your Apple ID’s iCloud settings. For any question or complaint, write to soporte@ritvo.app. You can also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es) or your local authority.

Changes

If what Ritvo does with data changes, we will update this page and its date before releasing the version that changes it.
